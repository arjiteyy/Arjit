A Love Letter Written in the Stars

For My Dearest Puntu, Mayalu & Ayuu — Timi Sadhai Bhari

Happy Birthday to my most beautiful person, my Puntu, my Mayalu, my Ayuu. ❤️🎂

Today is not just your birthday; it’s a reminder of how lucky I am that life brought you into mine. I still sometimes think about how everything started back in our school days, when we were just two people who didn’t know that one day we would become such an important part of each other’s lives. And now, somehow, it has been nearly two beautiful years of us. Nearly two years of memories, silly conversations, little fights, endless teasing, caring for each other, understanding each other, and loving each other in our own special way. ❤️

Ayuu, you have become so much more than just my girlfriend. You are the person I look for when I want to share something, the person who can make my worst days feel a little lighter, and the person whose happiness genuinely matters to me. You have a special place in my heart that nobody else could ever take. No matter how much I try to explain what you mean to me, words will always feel too small.

But even with the distance, one thing has never changed — you are still close to my heart. Maybe we cannot meet whenever we want, maybe we cannot spend every special moment together, but somehow you are still a part of my every day. I carry our memories with me everywhere. And honestly, sometimes even the smallest things remind me of you.

My Puntu, I want you to know that I am genuinely proud of you. I have watched you grow, and I hope I can continue watching you become the amazing person you are meant to be. You have your dreams, your studies, your future, and so many beautiful things waiting for you. I may not be able to solve every problem you face, but I promise that whenever you need someone, I will always want to be that person for you.

On your birthday, I don’t just wish you happiness for today. I wish you a life full of reasons to smile. I wish you success in everything you work hard for. I wish you confidence when you doubt yourself, strength when things become difficult, and people around you who genuinely appreciate the beautiful heart you have. I hope every dream you have slowly becomes reality. And more than anything, I hope you never forget how special you are.

And yes, I hope I get to be beside you for many of those future birthdays too. ❤️

We have already spent nearly two years together, but I don’t want our story to be measured only by the time that has passed. I want it to be measured by all the memories we have made and all the memories we still have to create. We have grown from school-day kids into two people with bigger dreams and bigger responsibilities, but I hope no matter how much life changes, we never forget the little version of us who first started this journey.

There will probably be more distance, more busy days, more misunderstandings, more arguments, and moments when things aren’t perfect. But I don’t expect our relationship to be perfect. I just want it to be real. I want us to keep choosing each other, keep understanding each other, keep talking, keep growing, and keep making memories together.

My Mayalu, thank you for every smile you have given me, every moment you have stayed, every little thing you have done for me, every time you have understood me, and even every little fight that somehow became another memory of us. Thank you for being you. ❤️

I hope when you read this, you smile even a little. And if you don’t, then I’m definitely going to blame you because I tried my best. 😂❤️

Happy Birthday once again, my Puntu, my Mayalu, my Ayuu. 🥹❤️🎂

Distance may keep us apart physically right now, but it can never erase everything we have shared. No matter how far you are, you will always have a very special place in my heart.

Keep smiling that beautiful smile.
Keep chasing your dreams.
Keep being the amazing girl you are.
And please remember that somewhere, there is someone who is always going to be cheering for you, missing you, caring about you, and loving you more than these words can ever explain.

Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️
I hope this new year of your life brings you everything your heart deserves.

I love you, Puntu.
I love you, Mayalu.
I love you, Ayuu. ❤️🥺

And here’s to us — to the memories behind us, the distance between us, and all the beautiful moments waiting ahead of us.

Happy Birthday, my beautiful girl. ❤️🎂🫶🏻

Forever & Always Yours,
Floating in Love ✨
